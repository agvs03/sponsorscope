Privacy notice

Last updated October 30, 2026

Without an account

Everything you enter — your resume, uploaded resume file, saved jobs and AI key — stays in your own browser (localStorage). Uploaded resumes are read on your device and never sent to us.

With an account

If you sign in, we store only what's needed to sync across your devices and send the alerts you ask for:

  • Your email address (from the sign-in link or Google).
  • Your parsed resume details as entered in the Resume Builder — name, contact fields, summary, skills, experience and education text. The original resume file is never uploaded or stored.
  • Jobs you save to your tracker and their status.
  • Saved searches for daily alerts, and when an alert was last sent.

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and alert emails are sent through Resend. Database rules ensure each account can read and change only its own data. We don't sell or share your data, show ads, or use it to train models.

Your controls

  • Delete my account (Account menu) permanently removes your account and everything above.
  • Every alert email has a one-click unsubscribe link; you can also turn alerts off in the Account menu.

Not advice

Sponsorship information comes from public DOL and USCIS disclosures and describes past filings, not a promise for any role. Nothing here is legal or immigration advice.
